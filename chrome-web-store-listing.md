# Chrome Web Store Listing — AdPulse

## Extension Name
AdPulse — Meta Ads Manager Dashboard

## Short Description (132 chars max)
Manage all your Facebook ad accounts, Business Managers & Fan Pages in one dashboard. Health checks, bulk actions, CSV exports.

## Detailed Description

Managing hundreds of Facebook ad accounts shouldn't require switching between dozens of tabs. AdPulse gives you a unified dashboard for every ad account, Business Manager, and Fan Page linked to your Facebook profile.

WHAT ADPULSE DOES:

- Multi-Account Dashboard — View and manage hundreds of ad accounts and Business Managers in one clean interface
- Account Health Checker — Instantly see which accounts are active, disabled, in review, or at risk of hitting spend limits
- Smart Search & Sort — Find any account instantly with real-time search across all your data
- CSV/Excel Export — Export your entire ad account portfolio with one click for client reporting
- Fan Page Manager — See all your pages with follower counts, categories, and publish status
- Auto-Refresh — Data refreshes automatically every 30 minutes in the background

BUILT FOR PHILIPPINE ADVERTISERS:
AdPulse is built specifically for Filipino media buyers, e-commerce sellers, and agencies who manage multiple ad accounts across Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, and other platforms.

PRIVACY FIRST — YOUR DATA STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER:
Unlike other tools, AdPulse stores ALL your data locally in your browser. We never send your Facebook data to any server. Your credentials, ad accounts, and business data never leave your device.

MINIMAL PERMISSIONS:
- We only access facebook.com, business.facebook.com, and adsmanager.facebook.com
- NO wildcard permissions
- NO access to other websites
- NO webRequest permission
- NO tabs permission

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Install AdPulse from the Chrome Web Store
2. Visit Facebook Ads Manager or Business Suite
3. AdPulse automatically detects your session
4. Click the AdPulse icon to see all your accounts

PRICING:

- Pro: PHP 1,499/month — unlimited accounts + bulk actions

All prices in Philippine Peso. GCash, Maya, and credit card accepted.
